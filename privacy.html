Privacy policy

WIT Job Monitor Drive Backup is a personal tool used by its owner. It uses Google Drive authorization to read and write the owner's job monitor backups: production and trial databases, posting exports, and a manifest in the private “WIT job monitor data” folder.

The Google Drive permission may technically allow access to other files in the owner's Drive. The backup program is configured to work only in the named folder. It does not scan, sell, or share other Drive files. The owner's backup data is stored in the owner's Google Drive, on the owner's computer, and temporarily in a private cloud runner while a scan runs. The OAuth credential is stored outside the source repository.

The owner controls the backups in Google Drive and can delete them there. The owner can revoke this app's Drive access in Google Account settings. The app does not use Drive data for advertising or sell it to anyone.
